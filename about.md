<span class="section-kicker">THE SHORT VERSION</span>

## Strategy with a human side.
{: #about-title }

I served as an infantry officer and program manager in the U.S. Army, then worked as an enterprise technology consultant at Deloitte Consulting and an associate program manager at SciTec. Those roles have involved building roadmaps, aligning stakeholders, and leading teams through high-stakes work.

I am pursuing a Master of Business Administration at the University of California, Berkeley, Haas School of Business, with certificates in Business Analytics and AI for Business projected for May 2027. At Haas, I serve as Co-President of the Veterans Club and VP of Allyship for the Asian Business Club.
